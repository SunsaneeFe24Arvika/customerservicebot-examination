TechNova AB – FAQ & Policy Document

1. Company Information
   TechNova AB is a Swedish e-commerce company that sells computers, phones and accessories.
   Website: [www.technova.se](http://www.technova.se)
   Customer Service: [support@technova.se](mailto:support@technova.se)
   Phone: 08–555 321 90
   Opening Hours: Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00

2. Order Questions
   How long is the delivery time?
   Standard delivery time is 2–5 business days. During peak season, delivery time can be up to 7 days.

Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, if your order has not been shipped. Contact customer service via email within 24 hours after ordering.

How do I know my order is confirmed?
You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after payment completion. Also check your spam folder if you don't receive an email.

3. Product Questions
   Do you offer warranty?
   Yes, all products have 1 year manufacturer warranty. Some brands, like NovaTech and QuantumGear, offer 2 year warranty.

What does the warranty cover?
The warranty covers manufacturing defects, but not physical damage or water damage.

Can I buy spare parts?
Yes, certain spare parts (such as chargers, batteries and cables) are available in our Spare Parts section on the website.

4. Return and Refund Policy
   Right of Withdrawal
   As a customer, you have 14 days right of withdrawal according to Swedish law. The product must be in new condition, in original packaging and unused.

How to return a product:

1. Contact [support@technova.se](mailto:support@technova.se) and provide your order number.
2. Wait for return form and instructions.
3. Send back the product within 14 days from return request.

Refund time:
When the item is received and approved, refund is made within 5 business days.

Return cost:
Return shipping fee of 49 SEK is deducted from the refund, unless the product is defective upon delivery.

5. Delivery and Shipping Questions
   Which shipping companies do you use?
   We collaborate with PostNord, DHL and Budbee. The customer can choose shipping method at checkout.

Can I track my order?
Yes, you receive a tracking number via email when the order is shipped.

Do you deliver outside Sweden?
Yes, to Denmark, Finland and Norway. Delivery time is then 5–10 business days.

6. Privacy Policy
   How do you handle personal data?
   TechNova follows GDPR. Personal data is only used for order processing, delivery and customer support.

Do you share information with third parties?
We only share information with payment services and shipping companies to fulfill the order.

How long is customer data stored?
Customer data is stored for 3 years after last purchase, then anonymized.

7. Technical Support
   What do I do if the product doesn't work?
   Contact customer support. In many cases, the problem can be solved via remote support or an update.

How do I file a complaint about a broken product?
Send an email to [support@technova.se](mailto:support@technova.se) with:

- Order number
- Description of the defect
- Photo of the product
  If the product is covered by warranty, a shipping label is sent at no cost.

8. Safety Recommendations

- Only charge products with original accessories.
- Do not expose electronics to water or high heat.
- Store lithium batteries at room temperature.

9. Environment and Sustainability
   TechNova AB works for sustainable e-commerce:

- We offer recycling of old products when purchasing new ones.
- Our packaging is 100% recyclable.
- We collaborate with GreenTech Recycling AB for collection of electronic waste.
